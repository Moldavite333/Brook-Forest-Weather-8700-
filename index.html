Glossary
Wet-bulb temperature +
The temperature air can cool toward through evaporation. During precipitation, wet-bulb temperature can be more useful than ordinary air temperature for judging rain-to-snow transitions.
Model spread +
How far apart the forecast models are. Tight clustering usually supports higher confidence; wide spread means the atmosphere or model handling is less certain.
Microclimate +
Local terrain, elevation, slope, trees, exposure and cold-air drainage can make your house behave differently from a nearby town forecast. The LOCAL page is designed to expose those differences.
