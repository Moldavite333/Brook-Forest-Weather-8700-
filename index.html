Sources / method
Numerical guidance: Open-Meteo public API exposing NOAA HRRR, NBM, NAM, GFS, ECMWF IFS, DWD ICON, Canadian GEM and UK Met Office global guidance. Elevation is forced to 2,652 m / 8,700 ft for statistical downscaling. Official forecast, alerts and observations: api.weather.gov. Radar: NOAA/NWS MRMS base-reflectivity map service. The model mean is kept separate from the official NWS forecast to avoid double-counting blended guidance.
